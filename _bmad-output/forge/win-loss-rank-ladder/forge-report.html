Win/loss rank ladder

Replace the Season Points ladder, where every finished match added SP, with one where losses cost SP, players can drop a division or a whole rank, and rank means who is actually best. Every open question was settled. Next step: bmad-correct-course.

The formula

SP change = base × margin × opponent (+5 to the Capot team) base wins pay more than losses cost (placeholder +30 / −20) margin 0.5 + (winner − loser points) ÷ target, capped 0.5–1.5 surrender = as if winners reached target · instant win = 1.5 opponent Elo-style expected result from the team-average SP gap every bot seat counts as the start of Gold

Locked decisions

Purpose

  • Rank is the true list of who's best. Losers sit below winners.
  • No matchmaking by rank. Honor and coin buy-in still gate rooms.

Ladder

  • Iron through Diamond: 3 divisions each, with 3 highest (Gold 1 < 2 < 3).
  • Master and Grandmaster are single.
  • Players can drop through divisions and ranks freely. SP never goes below 0.
  • Everyone restarts at Iron 0 each quarter.

Removed from today's formula

  • +50 for finishing
  • Flat +100 for winning
  • Game points ÷ 10 (why SP looked like XP)
  • +50 Capot bonus to all four seats

Abandonment

  • Abandoner: a fixed loss of 2× the worst possible loss.
  • Teammate: half the loss a surrender at that moment would have cost, in every room type, with the reason shown on screen.
  • Opponents: a normal win, scored as a surrender.

Tuning

  • First build step: an offline script runs Q3 2026's matches through the formula.
  • A player winning 50% reaches Silver within about 20 matches.
  • Curve steepness is calculated from the 85% target. Division borders are split evenly.

Cutover

  • Release 2026-10-01 (Q4 starts 00:00 UTC). The release resets Q4 to 0.
  • Q3 and earlier stay archived as flat ranks, with no division.

Solo players against bots

Bot matches count for rank. A solo player settles at the rank that matches their long-run win rate against bots, so Grandmaster takes real dominance, not just a lot of matches.

Deferred

  • Shields against dropping a division
  • Remove bot matches from rank
  • Carry part of last season's SP over

Rejected, and why

  • Zero-sum ladder starting at Iron 0: the typical player gets stuck in Iron.
  • Mid-ladder season start: new players get a rank they didn't earn.
  • Linear opponent gap: can't make Grandmaster reachable through bots without rewarding volume.
  • Cancel the teammate's loss on abandon: lets a second account take losses for a main.
  • Full loss in private rooms, half in Quick Play: one rule everywhere is simpler.
  • Showing each player's share of bot matches: not wanted.
  • Replay Q4 / map old SP onto divisions / wait until 2027 Q1: none needed with a boundary release and a reset.

Weak points that survived

Quick Play

No honor gate and random partners, so the teammate penalty teaches less there.

The 0 floor

A loss can't go below 0, so SP quietly leaks into the system from the bottom.

Bot value

Start of Gold was accepted with low conviction. It's one constant, to retune with data.
