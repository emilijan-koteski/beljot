- (note) Prior rationale (sprint-change-proposal-2026-04-18): ELO/ranked rejected for a closed-circle, room-first audience; coins chosen as the skill/stakes signal (losers net -S); 'Coin + ELO coexistence' rejected because two competitive signals would confuse. Since then Quick Play matches strangers and bot-fills empty seats (commit 83edf47).
- (decision) April 2026 rejection was about matchmaking gated by rank (rooms already gated by honor + coin buy-in). Demotion was never meant to be dropped; the flat participation ladder over-read that proposal. Correct Course must record the reversal explicitly, since the proposal text lists 'scaled penalties' as retired.
- (lock) Rank's purpose: the real list of who is best in the game. It must be accurate: players who lose sit below players who win.
- (lock) No matchmaking by rank. Rooms stay gated only by honor and coin buy-in.
- (decision) SP change depends on the score margin (not a flat amount).
- (lock) Division numbering: 3 is highest (Gold 1 < Gold 2 < Gold 3). Iron through Diamond get 3 divisions; Master and Grandmaster are single.
- (decision) No demotion protection for now: players can fall through divisions and whole ranks freely. DEFERRED: shields that protect against dropping a whole division.
- (decision) Matches with bots count for rank for now (player base too small). DEFERRED: remove bot-filled matches from rank later.
- (assumption) Abandoning must be punishing, more than a loss; user asked for a recommended amount.
- (lock) Every SP change is scaled by opponent strength: the gap between the two teams' average SP (visible SP only, no hidden rating). Beating stronger teams pays more, losing to weaker teams costs more (e.g. ~1.5x at +1000 gap, ~0.5x at -1000). This stops grinding against weak tables, since there is no rank matchmaking. Applied on top of the score-margin term.
- (lock) Season reset stays: everyone restarts at Iron 0 each season. Wins pay somewhat more than losses cost, so an average player creeps up; rank thresholds are set so an average season ends mid-ladder.
- (decision) DEFERRED: carry part of last season's SP into the next one (e.g. keep the rank, or drop one or two ranks below it).
- (note) Population today: ~80 registered players, ~10 active; the 2 most active play alone with bots. The ladder must let solo-with-bots players climb.
- (crack) Bots have no SP. If a bot counts as 0 SP in the opponent scaling, players who mostly play bots gain almost nothing per win once above Iron and lose heavily per loss, so they stall low every season.
- (decision) Every bot seat counts as a fixed SP value in the middle of the ladder (start of Gold) for opponent scaling. Gives a fast climb out of Iron after the reset and a natural ceiling for bot-only players; Master/Grandmaster need wins over real high-ranked players. It is a single tunable constant (user accepted with low conviction; retune once real data exists). Tuning constraint: the win reduction against weaker teams must go well below 0.5x, or anyone beating bots > 2/3 of the time climbs forever.
- (lock) Capot: small flat bonus (e.g. +5 SP) only for the team that made the Capot. The old +50 to all four seats is removed.
- (decision) Base formula direction: SP change = base x margin x opponent. Margin = 0.5 + (winner - loser points) / target (501/1001), capped 0.5-1.5. Surrender scored as if winners reached the target. Instant win = max margin. Removed: +50 completion, flat +100 win, game points / 10 (the XP-like term), +50 Capot for all. Both teammates get the same change.
- (crack) User wants solo-vs-bots players able to reach Grandmaster. This conflicts with the rank-is-who-is-best lock and the earlier 'GM needs real high-ranked humans' reasoning, unless GM via bots requires genuine dominance rather than volume.
- (direction) Proposed fix: make the opponent scaling Elo-style (expected result from the SP gap on a log curve). With bots fixed at the start of Gold, a bot-only player settles at a rank set by their sustained win rate vs bots: 50% -> Gold, higher win rate -> higher, no hard ceiling. Constants (K, curve scale, anchor, thresholds, win bonus) need a simulation to tune convergence speed and the win-rate-to-rank table.
- (lock) Opponent scaling uses the Elo-style expected-result curve (replaces the linear gap example). Solo players vs bots can reach Grandmaster; tune so a sustained ~85% win rate vs bots settles at the Grandmaster floor (roughly 50% Gold, 60% Platinum, 70% Diamond, 80% Master). Exact constants come from a simulation.
- (kill) Rejected: showing each player's share of ranked matches played with bots on profile/leaderboard. Not wanted.
- (decision) Abandoner loses a fixed 2x the worst possible loss (always worse than losing by any margin). Opponents get a normal win scored like a surrender (as if they reached the target). Abandoner = only the seat whose reconnect window expired, as today.
- (decision) The abandoner's teammate also takes a loss (so players learn to avoid that partner / honor level), but lighter than the abandoner. Rejected: cancelling the teammate's loss (lets a second account take losses for a main).
- (crack) Quick Play rooms have no honor gate (MinHonor 0) and seat strangers, so a Quick Play teammate could not choose their partner; the 'learn who not to play with' reason only fully applies to private rooms.
- (lock) Abandoner's teammate loses half of what a surrender at that moment would have cost them. Same rule in private rooms and Quick Play. Match-end screen should show why (e.g. 'Partner abandoned: -12 SP (half loss)').
- (lock) Cutover: release on the season boundary (2026 Q4 starts 2026-10-01 00:00 UTC), and the release resets Q4 SP to 0 for everyone so Q4 is scored entirely by the new formula. The reset also covers any matches played between 00:00 UTC and the actual deploy.
- (note) Q3 2026 and earlier stay archived under the old flat tiers (no division). Profile archive must render old seasons without a division.
- (kill) Rejected cutover options: replaying Q4 (nothing to replay if released on the boundary), mapping old SP onto divisions (old SP includes points earned by losing), waiting until 2027 Q1.
- (lock) Tuning: first build step is an offline (unshipped) script that runs Q3 2026 stored matches through the new formula. Targets: sustained 85% vs bots settles at the Grandmaster floor; a 50% player reaches Silver within ~20 matches. Curve steepness is derived from the 85% target and bot value; division borders split evenly within each rank. Fallback if short on time: ship derived values + placeholders, retune after a few weeks of Q4.
